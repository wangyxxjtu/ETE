#just a quick training and testing guidance for N4News dataset, VisualNews and GoodNews can be done in the same way
 
step 1: download the N4News dataset via this link: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OS1fXwZ1Vsj70lEQajccyssxQRYp5X9D/view?usp=share_link

step 2: place the dataset to /your/data/path/
the structure is :
     N24News
           --imgs
           --news

step 3: download our generated explanation text: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1kbwiZIATA8vsGYa23XS-u0WFZlR99grN?usp=sharing
       and place them into /your/data/path/N24News/news/

step 3: specific the dataset path as /your/data/path/ in run.sh

step 4: sh run.sh
